# Feature status and pending programme-owner decisions

Status as of 6 Nov 2026 (after the functional audit – see [FUNCTIONAL_AUDIT.md](FUNCTIONAL_AUDIT.md)). The live version of this view is in the application under **Programme status & decisions**
(`GET /api/v1/system/feature-status`, `GET /api/v1/programme-decisions`), which also counts approvals as they happen.

Legend: **Implemented** – working and tested · **Configurable** – working; behaviour set by programme configuration ·
**Awaiting approval** – working with a provisional treatment until the programme owner decides · **Not started**.

| Integrations | FarmFuture Water Balance Engine (Workstream 2) | Implemented – test access | Live read-only pull (`farmfuture_water_engine`): daily water account and irrigation plan per identity-mapped farm, stored as *calculated* water-balance records. Tested with a 7-day test token; production needs a FarmFuture partner account |
| Integrations | FarmFuture soil & microclimate sensors (Workstream 4) | Implemented – test access | Live read-only pull (`farmfuture_soil_microclimate`): *Import devices* registers each SoilSync / MicroClime device on its linked plot; the pull stores the daily min / max / mean of soil moisture (two depths), soil temperature, pH, EC, N, P, K, air temperature, humidity, rain, pressure, wind, leaf wetness and light, plus soil lab reports that name a laboratory. Offline all-zero days are skipped. N/P/K in kg/ha and EC in µS/cm as labelled in the FarmFuture app; values match the FarmFuture app exactly (checked 6 Nov 2026). Setting `series` chooses the app, raw, calibrated or lab-anchored series and every reading names its series; readings outside the physically plausible range are kept as *exceptions* for an analyst. `sensor_history_days` (default 30) fills in earlier days once. Each device's installed position (FarmFuture lat / long, refreshed by *Import devices*; a position entered in the platform is kept) – shown as a square on the farm map and on the device page, coloured by whether it is reporting; exact positions need `farmer.read_pii`, and a device more than 2 km from its plot's point is listed with a warning instead of being drawn (one such FarmFuture position found, 198 km off). Each FarmFuture device page also shows its readings *as the FarmFuture app shows them*, read live and never stored: the Sensors-page card (GetAllSensorsLatestData – FarmFuture's latest reading before its own correction, with its labels, rounding and dry-soil N/P/K rule) and the Farm Insights 7-day average / low / high (GetFarmStats). Daily *device health* snapshots (battery, signal, last reading) and Analogue Weather stations (latest temperature, humidity, wind, leaf wetness, light). The IoT devices page shows data days in the last 30 days, battery and signal; the dashboard counts devices reporting in the last 7 days. FarmFuture's get-advisory (a POST threshold check) was tested and is not imported (SOIL_SENSOR_THRESHOLDS). Leaf-wetness unit and the reporting series pending (FARMFUTURE_SENSOR_UNITS) |
| Integrations | FarmFuture on-farm weather & forecast (Workstream 1) | Implemented – test access | Live read-only pull (`farmfuture_weather`): daily rain, max / min temperature and mean humidity from the MicroClime stations as *climate observations* (linked to the station device), and FarmFuture's 5-day forecast per located farm as *weather forecasts* (whole India-time days only, kelvin converted to °C; the dataset names the forecast location FarmFuture used). `rain_history_days` (default 365) fills in the station's earlier daily rain (days not reported stay missing, never 0 mm; 2026 totals match FarmFuture's rain report). A station listed on two FarmFuture farms stays on the plot its device is registered on. FarmFuture publishes no climate-risk (drought / heat / excess-rain) API, so no risk levels are created |
| Climate | Rainfall status vs 30-year normal (Workstream 1) | Implemented – provisional bands | Platform calculation source `platform_rainfall_status`: per plot and month, station rain since 1 January against the plot's 30-year normal (CHIRPS 1996–2025 via the FarmFuture farm climate profile, stored as *30-year climate normals*), share of usual rain, missing rain and status band (extra / usual / little less / much less / very much less – FarmFuture's bands, setting `climate.rainfall_status_bands`). Days without a station reading are missing, never dry; below 80 % measured days the status is *insufficient data* (`climate.rainfall_status_min_coverage_pct`). Reproduces FarmFuture's rain report exactly (Palthope estate, 1 Jan – 5 Nov 2026: 1,663.4 of 2,039.2 mm, 81.6 %, 277/309 days). Shown on the dashboard, the farm map (colour layer and plot panel) and the Climate module. Pending RAINFALL_STATUS_BANDS |
| Integrations | FarmFuture pest & disease scouting (Workstream 3) | Implemented – test access | Live read-only pull (`farmfuture_pest_scouting`): app scouting records → scouting / inspection records (plants inspected / with signs, incidence calculated). Phone numbers, notes, photos and GPS points are not imported |
| Workstreams | Farm pages mirroring the FarmFuture app (Workstreams 1–5) | Implemented – test access | `/climate` (dashboard weather station, station rain season card, weather warnings, Farm Insights weather cards, Missing Rain Report), `/water` (Irrigation Planning: advice, tiles, blocks, 7-day outlook), `/pest-forecast` (Farm Health Inspection plan: satellite exposure map, areas and points, station/sun overlay, pest photos, what to do, per-zone screening), `/soil` (Farm Insights: period picker, soil and weather cards with crop thresholds, 15 field insights), `/suitability` (Farm Profile, EUDR screening, zone NDVI). FarmFuture's own calculation code is copied unchanged into the frontend (`features/*/ff`, reference copies of agricentral-web) and run on FarmFuture's data read live through `/farm-health/*` (GET only, cached, nothing stored). Exact locations (outline, zones, cells, positions) only with `farmer.read_pii`; scouting notes, photos and recorders and profile outlines are never passed on. Menu items hidden 10 Nov 2026: Practice adoption, Demonstration sites, Pest surveillance clusters, Community resource persons (routes kept) |
| Integrations | FarmFuture Farm Health pest & disease screen (Workstream 3) | Implemented – test access | Part of the same pull (config `risk_screen`, on by default): FarmFuture's own screening rules (coffee-screen-2026-09-v2 – web app `wsb/models`, ported to `coffee_pest_screen.py` because FarmFuture runs them in the browser and has no API for the result) run on the farm's FarmFuture 3-hourly forecast, MicroClime station reading, farm calendar and scouting → one *pest/disease risk estimate* per day for white stem borer, leaf rust, black rot, berry borer, shot-hole borer, mealybug and root health (low = routine check, high = inspect now, very high = signs found), with FarmFuture's status, reasons, what to check and missing evidence. Host from the devices' registered crops (else the plot crop); farms registered only for other crops (tea, mango) are skipped, as in FarmFuture. A topic FarmFuture cannot judge (no fresh forecast, host unknown) gets no estimate. Farm-level, whereas FarmFuture screens one block at a time |
| Integrations | FarmFuture satellite block greenness and site profile (Workstream 5) | Implemented – test access | Live read-only pull (`farmfuture_satellite`): per-block NDVI mean / min / max, NDMI mean and declining cells from FarmFuture's latest Sentinel-2 pass, as vegetation-index records; with `site_profile`, each farm's *site characteristics* – elevation, slope, aspect, drainage, frost risk, soil texture / pH / organic carbon / water holding, RUSLE soil loss and erosion class, wetness index (assessment; no outline or points stored) |
